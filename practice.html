Officer Training Samples

Sample exercises for patrol officers and detectives. Use the applicable agency policy and coordinate device handling with the forensic examiner.

Preserve a Mobile Device in the Field

Scenario. An officer lawfully collects a phone with a dark screen and cannot tell whether it is asleep or powered off.

  1. Record where the phone was found, its visible condition and connections, and who handled it.
  2. Contact the examiner for a plan that accounts for device state, network isolation and power.
  3. Record any intervention and document the transfer of custody.

Discussion prompt. What changes if the screen is visibly on, the battery is low, or the device is damaged?

Proposed field kit. Stock each detective with multiple small, approved Faraday bags and each patrol officer or squad car with at least one. Check the bags regularly and replace damaged ones.

Write a Detective-to-Lab Intake Request

Scenario. In a fictional invoice fraud case, the investigator wants to determine whether a payment instruction arrived during a defined period.

  1. State the item and the specific investigative question.
  2. Attach applicable authority and identify the authorized account, date range and data types.
  3. Document device condition, preservation steps, custody, urgency and a case contact.
  4. Confirm receipt and agree with the examiner on the expected deliverable.

Example request. Identify messages or attachments in the authorized account during the specified dates that contain the disputed payment instructions. Report the source and time basis for each finding.

Review Digital Findings and Prepare the Handoff

Scenario. A fictional forensic report shows a relevant message and a timestamp. The source and time zone need confirmation.

  1. Record the artifact reference and distinguish what the source shows from the investigative inference.
  2. Refer material observations back to the examiner to verify the source, time basis and limitations.
  3. Provide the authorized report and supporting references to the prosecutor through the approved process.

Discussion prompt. Which part of the proposed conclusion can you support with the source record, and what needs independent corroboration?
